Use an absolute Windows path or UNC share. This path is accessed by the Windows account running Python/Waitress, rather than your browser or IIS user. Grant that account Modify permissions on the root and its subfolders; for UNC storage, grant both share and NTFS access.

Backups and restoration

External customer document bytes are not included in Download full backup or automatic database/attachment backups. Those backups include the metadata, mappings and audit history.

For a consistent complete backup: stop the Workplace/Waitress service, take the normal full backup, and copy the entire external document root (including hidden ownership/journal files) while the service remains stopped. Store this pair together. Restore both from the same backup point before restarting. Keep the Windows service account and folder permissions.

Do not modify customer documents directly on disk or permit untrusted OS writers. Links, junctions and reparse points are rejected. Downloads are attachments and files are not executed or extracted by Workplace.

Existing allocated roots are not silently switched to another folder. Follow the Version 15 offline backup/restore procedure before relocating storage.
