Quotations

Open quotations to use Copy link, validity countdowns and Favorite on each quotation. Expiring Soon highlights the next seven days.

Inside a saved quotation, use Controls → More for Internal notes, Pre-send check and Approval & reminders. Notes stay internal and never appear in customer exports.

Your shortcuts

Favorites & recently viewed keeps your own quotation/customer favorites and last ten opened records. Your access permissions apply.

Use the search bar or Ctrl+K to find quotations, customers, projects and permitted document filenames.

Approvals & sending

Pending approvals shows reviews that need your action. Open a quotation’s Approval page for Remind Pending Approvers. A one-hour cooldown prevents repeated requests.

The Pre-send check verifies the customer, validity, pricing, required approvals and approved PDF before the existing Sent to Client step.

Customers & dashboard

Open a customer and select Activity timeline for quotation, approval, sent and document events.

The dashboard includes active quotations, pending approvals, expiring quotations, sent-this-month counts and Needs Your Attention.
